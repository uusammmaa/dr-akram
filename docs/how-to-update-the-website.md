# How to update the website

This guide is for Prof. Dr. Mian Muhammad Akram. The public site is [drmianakram.com](https://drmianakram.com). You change the words and pictures yourself. You do not need a developer for ordinary updates.

The editing screen is in English. The website itself is in English and Urdu.

## Open the editing screen

1. Go to [drmianakram.com/studio](https://drmianakram.com/studio).
2. Sign in with the account that was set up for you.
3. The left menu is the list of sections.

A short tour may start the first time. You can open **Help** in the left menu any time.

## Save and publish

Typing is not enough. Visitors see a change only after you publish it.

1. Edit the fields.
2. Press **Publish** at the bottom.
3. Wait about one minute, then refresh the website.

**Unpublish** hides that item from the website. It does not delete it.

## What each section is for

| Left menu | What it changes on the website |
|---|---|
| Site Settings | Your photo, phone, WhatsApp, email, and the short line under your name |
| About Page | Your biography and the career timeline |
| Books → My Books | Books you wrote |
| Books → Recommended Reading | Books you suggest other people read |
| Lectures | YouTube videos |
| Honors & Awards | Shields, trophies, and certificates |
| Afkar-e-Muallim | Magazine issues |
| Organizations | The organizations on the About page |
| Q&A → Inbox | New questions from visitors. They are not public yet |
| Q&A → Published FAQ | Answers that visitors can read |
| Help | Short reminders for these same tasks |

Fill both the English and Urdu fields when you have both. If Urdu is empty, the Urdu page uses the English text.

## Change your photo, phone, or email

1. Open **Site Settings**.
2. Open **Site Settings** again in that list.
3. Upload a photo under **Portrait**, or edit **Phone**, **WhatsApp**, and **Email**.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Change your biography or career timeline

1. Open **Site Settings → About Page**.
2. Edit the biography.
3. Under career milestones, add a year, a title, and a short description. Use the handles to put them in order.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Add a book you wrote

1. Open **Books → My Books**.
2. Press **Create**.
3. Enter the title, level (for example M.A. or General), and a short description.
4. Upload the cover.
5. If there is a place to buy it, paste that link.
6. Turn on **Featured on Homepage** only if this book should appear on the home page.
7. Press **Publish**.

## Suggest a book for others to read

1. Open **Books → Recommended Reading**.
2. Press **Create**.
3. Enter the title, the author’s name, the topic, and a note on why it is worth reading.
4. Upload the cover and press **Publish**.

## Add a lecture

The lecture must already be on YouTube.

1. Open the video on YouTube.
2. Copy the address from the browser. It looks like `https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=...` or `https://youtu.be/...`.
3. Open **Lectures** and press **Create**.
4. Paste that address into **YouTube URL**. Add the title.
5. Press **Publish**.

A YouTube search page address will not play on the website. Use the address of one video.

## Add a shield, trophy, or certificate

1. Open **Honors & Awards**.
2. Press **Create**.
3. Upload the photo. Add the name, type, organization, event, and year.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Add a magazine issue

1. Open **Afkar-e-Muallim**.
2. Press **Create**.
3. Upload the cover. Add the month and year, and either a PDF or a link.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Add an organization

1. Open **Organizations**.
2. Press **Create**.
3. Add the name, your role, and whether it is current or former. Upload the logo if you have one.
4. Press **Publish**.

## Answer a question from the website

1. Open **Q&A → Inbox (pending)**.
2. Open the question.
3. Write the answer in English and in Urdu.
4. Set **Status** to **Published** if it should appear on the website. Set it to **Private** if you answered the person directly and do not want it public.
5. Press **Publish**.

A question stays hidden until the status is **Published**.

## If something goes wrong

Write down which section you were in and what you pressed. Then contact your son on WhatsApp. Do not delete a section from the left menu.
